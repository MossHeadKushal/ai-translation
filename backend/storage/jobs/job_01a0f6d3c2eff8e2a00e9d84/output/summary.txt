KEY INSIGHTS & SUMMARY POINTS:
• Self-Hosted Architecture: Operates entirely on local hardware with zero external third-party API dependencies or fees.
• 100% Anonymous: Eliminates login barriers, user accounts, and telemetry tracking for strict data confidentiality.
• Multi-Modal AI Tools: Comprehensive toolchain covering Image-to-3D, Speech-to-Text, Subtitles, OCR, Translation, and TTS.
• Automated Data Purge: Ephemeral workspaces and temporary files are automatically cleaned up after the retention window.
• Real-Time Monitoring: Streaming Server-Sent Events (SSE) provide live progress and worker pool telemetry.
